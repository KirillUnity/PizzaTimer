# Proposal

## Why

Pause still needs a way to finish the task. The user wants one centered **button** «Завершить задачу», not a text link, and no «Продолжить» on the face.

## What Changes

- While a break is open, show one filled Finish-task button, centered above the dial.
- Do not show Continue on the face. Resume stays on the hub control.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- (none)

### Modified Capabilities

- `flow-session`: one centered Finish button; no Continue on the face.

## Impact

- `DayScreen` break actions.
