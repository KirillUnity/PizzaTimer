# Proposal: Introduction as the product thesis

## Why
The first screens are a manual: leftover shares, 5:3:2, immutable blocks. An empty column leaves the copy at the top, and Skip / Next sit under the system navigation bar. Mission §2 is the bet a person should see on open: a clear queue, led by the experience of the work, not a whip. §6.3 and §6.4 say how that bet looks on the day.

## What Changes
- Four introduction pages, in order: the queue, one place, one quiet step, one next line.
- Each page keeps a short kicker, a headline, one paragraph, and a paper figure in the space that is empty today.
- Skip and Next stay above the system bars. Skip and the last page still open the day.
- No leftover ratios, streaks, points, or a red countdown on these pages.

## Capabilities
- `flow-session`: the introduction states the thesis and stays tappable.

## Impact
`OnboardingScreen`, introduction strings, and four static figures. Practice lessons, the day dial, and the flow session are unchanged.

## Preservation
Finishing or skipping still marks the introduction seen and opens the day. The dispositional form stays absent. Task list, flow session, and the day dial stay intact.
