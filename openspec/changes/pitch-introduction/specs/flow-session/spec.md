## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Introduction states the thesis
The introduction MUST be four pages in this order: a clear queue without a whip, the day as one place with one front, one quiet step with a single sound at its edge, and one next line after the step. Each page MUST show a headline, one short paragraph, and a figure. Skip and Next MUST remain above the system navigation bar. The pages MUST NOT explain leftover shares, streaks, points, or a red countdown. Finishing or skipping MUST still open the day.

#### Scenario: First page is the queue
- **WHEN** the introduction opens
- **THEN** the first page states what to do now and what comes next, and that a streak, shame, and a red countdown are not the lead

#### Scenario: Controls clear the navigation bar
- **WHEN** the introduction is open on a phone with a system navigation bar
- **THEN** Skip and Next are fully visible above that bar
