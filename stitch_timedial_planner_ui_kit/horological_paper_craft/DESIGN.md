## Brand & Style

This design system synthesizes traditional horology with warm editorial paper craft, optimized for a native Android Material 3 environment. It bridges the tactile precision of mechanical chronographs, bespoke watch dials, and the organic calm of physical stationery.

### Personality & Tone
- **Calm & Deliberate:** Replaces anxiety-inducing digital timers with measured, analog circular progress.
- **Instrumental Tactility:** Interfaces feel mechanically calibrated yet organic—reminiscent of engraved dial plates, heavy bond paper, and saturated fountain pen inks.
- **Quiet Utility:** Uncluttered, ergonomic, and respectful of cognitive bandwidth, relying on spatial clarity and material rhythm over decorative noise.

### Visual Aesthetic
A synthesis of **Tactile Physical Metaphors** and modern **Material 3 Expressive**. Surfaces evoke soft vellum and warm charcoal slate. Visual priority centers on a high-precision 24-hour circular dial, balanced with humanist sans typography and structural 8dp spacing rhythm.

## Colors

The palette reproduces physical stationery and horological instrumentation. High-contrast indicators sit over soft, non-glare paper substrates.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#E0533C` - Terracotta Now Marker):** Dedicated strictly to temporal immediacy—the current time needle, live countdown ring, and primary interactive execution points.
- **Secondary (`#EADBCE` - Sand Beige):** Represents temporal void, unallocated intervals, rest blocks, and quiet container fills.
- **Tertiary (`#2E7D52` - Deep Emerald):** Focal productivity states, high-priority work segments, and constructive confirmation triggers.
- **Neutral Surface & Ink:**
  - Light Mode Canvas: `#FBF9F5` (Warm Paper vellum) with `#1C1B18` (Iron gall ink) for primary typography.
  - Dark Mode Canvas: `#161513` (Deep Charcoal Slate) with `#EDE8DF` (Bleached bone ink) for primary typography.

### Functional Chrono & State Tones
- **Completed Segments:** `#8E8B82` (Warm Gray) across track fills, subdued text, and past events.
- **Category Inks (User Task Rings & Badges):**
  - Focus / Deep Work: Emerald (`#2E7D52`)
  - Creative / Strategy: Ochre (`#E59819`)
  - Admin / Logistics: Cobalt (`#2563EB`)
  - Personal / Wellness: Plum (`#7C3AED`)
  - Urgent / Deadline: Rose (`#DB2777`)

### Surface Tiering
- `surface-dim`: `#F2EFE9` (Light) / `#11100F` (Dark)
- `surface`: `#FBF9F5` (Light) / `#161513` (Dark)
- `surface-bright`: `#FFFFFF` (Light) / `#22201D` (Dark)
- `surface-container-low`: `#F5F2EB` (Light) / `#1C1A18` (Dark)
- `surface-container`: `#EFEBE2` (Light) / `#23211E` (Dark)
- `surface-container-high`: `#E9E4D9` (Light) / `#2A2723` (Dark)

## Typography

The typography unites **Domine** (a sturdy, authoritative slab-serif) with **Plus Jakarta Sans** (a clean, geometric humanist sans).

### Dial Numerals & Editorial Displays
- **Domine** renders watch dial ticks, roman/arabic chapter rings, remaining duration callouts, and key editorial screen titles. The bracketed serifs and high x-height ground numeric information with the gravity of calibrated instrument bezels.
- Use tabular lining figures for numbers within the clock dial and countdown matrices to preserve absolute geometric balance during real-time movement.

### Operational UI & Metadata
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** manages task descriptions, metadata labels, timing readouts, chips, and navigational anchors.
- All micro-labels (`label-sm`, `label-md`) use uppercase formatting with widened letter spacing (`+0.05em` to `+0.08em`) to mimic stamped metal watch case hallmarks.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout geometry follows an explicit **8dp baseline grid** (with 4dp sub-steps for fine dial subdivisions). Touch ergonomics center around Android accessibility criteria with mandatory 48dp minimum interactive target boundaries.

### Form Factor Adaptations
- **Compact (< 600dp - Mobile):** Single-column layout. The dial dominates the upper 55% of the viewport; tasks and breakdown cards dock into a lower scrollable sheet. Outer canvas margins are fixed at `16dp` (`margin-compact`), with a `48dp` bottom navigation bar clearance.
- **Medium (600dp - 839dp - Foldables / Small Tablets):** Two-column split layout. Dial stays pinned left inside a 1:1 square module; time inventory list populates the right panel with `16dp` gutters.
- **Expanded (>= 840dp - Tablets / Desktop):** Three-column structural layout with docked navigation rail. Dial stays centrally aligned in an interactive command staging area flanked by unassigned backlogs and live segment summaries. Outer margins scale to `32dp` (`margin-expanded`).

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than synthetic blue-tinted drop shadows, elevation relies on **tonal container layering** blended with soft, warm-umber ambient occlusion shadows.

### Material 3 Surface Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** `surface` (`#FBF9F5`). The primary paper canvas. Clock dials are recessed into this plane via an inset border (`1dp solid #E5E0D6`).
- **Level 1 (Card Containers & Inactive Slots):** `surface-container-low` (`#F5F2EB`). Shadow: `0px 1px 3px rgba(28, 27, 24, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Slices & Selected Items):** `surface-container` (`#EFEBE2`). Shadow: `0px 2px 6px rgba(28, 27, 24, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Controls, Menus & FAB):** `surface-container-high` (`#E9E4D9`). Shadow: `0px 4px 12px rgba(28, 27, 24, 0.12)`.
- **Level 4 (Modal Sheets & Time Scrubbers):** `surface-bright`. Shadow: `0px 8px 24px rgba(28, 27, 24, 0.16)`.

### Dial Depth & Tactility
The circular dial utilizes an engraved aesthetic: radial ticks cut into the backdrop with low-opacity borders (`#1C1B18` at 12%), while the current-time terracotta indicator needle floats above the surface with a distinct `0px 2px 8px rgba(224, 83, 60, 0.35)` directional cast.

## Shapes

The design system uses **Roundedness Level 2** (8px / 0.5rem base radius). Corner curvature reflects classic pebble-smooth watch cases, rounded bezel rings, and organic edge cuts of heavyweight paper.

### Shape Mappings
- **Circular Components (Full Radius):** Dial backdrops, the circular slice scrubber head, time-allocation tags, and round icon buttons use `rounded-full` (9999px).
- **Small Containers & Chips:** `8px` (`0.5rem`) for compact tags, time ticks, and inputs.
- **Medium Cards & List Modules:** `16px` (`1rem`) for task cards, duration summaries, and timeline list items.
- **Large Surfaces & Sheets:** `24px` (`1.5rem`) for bottom sheets, quick-log drawers, and modal overlays.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Log / Commit Task):** Terracotta fill (`#E0533C`), bone text (`#FFFFFF`), `8px` radius, `48dp` fixed height. Active states trigger a warm darken scale (`#C8442F`).
- **Secondary Action (Rest Slice / Quick Pause):** Sand Beige fill (`#EADBCE`), ink text (`#1C1B18`), subtle border (`1dp solid #D7C7B7`).
- **Ghost / Chrono Control:** Transparent background, `1dp solid #8E8B82` (30% opacity), `1C1B18` text, full-rounded pill shape.

### The Analog Dial (Core Instrument)
- Circular gauge partitioned into 24-hour or 60-minute slices.
- Unscheduled blocks render in Sand Beige (`#EADBCE`); completed blocks transition into Warm Gray (`#8E8B82`).
- Active segment pulses with a 1.5dp inner highlight in the user task's designated hue.
- Dial center features tabular numeral readouts in Domine display typography.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- Height: `32dp`, radius: `8px`.
- Inactive: Background `surface-container-low`, text `neutral` (`#1C1B18`), border `1dp solid #E5E0D6`.
- Active: Background in task ink (Emerald, Ochre, Cobalt, Plum, Rose), text `#FFFFFF`, elevation level 1.

### Cards & Task Rows
- Task list items are modular strips (`56dp` height) set on `surface-container-low` with an `8px` radius.
- Saturated task hue is indicated via an architectural 4dp left border edge (fountain pen ink dip indicator), leaving the rest of the card clean paper.
- Trailing duration text uses tabular lining numbers for vertical scanning.

### Input Fields & Pickers
- Time entry fields use an engraved, outlined style: background `surface-container-low`, `1dp` border in `#D7C7B7`, focusing to `#E0533C` with a 2dp stroke.
- Text selection handles, cursor, and time scrubbers use Terracotta (`#E0533C`).

### Checkboxes & Segment Toggles
- Custom dial-bezel checkboxes: `20dp` squircle (`4dp` radius).
- Unchecked: `2dp` outline in `#8E8B82`.
- Checked: Solid `#1C1B18` fill with an off-white `#FBF9F5` tick mark, accompanied by an immediate haptic tick (imitating a mechanical chronograph crown click).
